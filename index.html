Magic Merge

A cozy merge puzzle with Lily, the fairy who lives in the corner of your screen.

Download on the App Store

Magic Merge is a cozy merge puzzle for iOS where every drag is a tiny spell. You combine items across nine merge lines and twelve tiers (108 items total), tend a quiet garden on a second tab, and slowly piece together a 27-episode story told by three small voices — Lily the fairy, Root the gardener, and Saffi the owl.

Monetization is honest mid-tier f2p: energy that regenerates, optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch), removable interstitials between level-ups, and a one-time $9.99 Remove Ads IAP. What's gone from the genre standard: no PvP, no level walls, no FOMO events, no banner ads. Built in three months by a solo developer in Illinois, entirely in native SwiftUI for iOS 17+.

Lily who pays attention

A fairy companion who notices when you've been still, gently flies to a useful pair, and remembers your habits.

Native iOS craft

SwiftUI, Live Activity, widgets, App Intents, and four passes on the Core Haptics engine.

Free, with optional Remove Ads

One-time $9.99 IAP removes both rewarded and interstitial ads forever. Cosmetic outfit IAP for Lily is separate.
